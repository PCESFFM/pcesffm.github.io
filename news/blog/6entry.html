Snow Ball

A night of celebration, dancing and unforgettable memories

6 February, 2026

Our school recently hosted a fantastic Snow Ball for students in S4 to S6. For one evening, the school was transformed into an elegant and vibrant venue, creating a special atmosphere for everyone who attended. The event brought together pupils for a night of celebration, laughter and unforgettable memories.

A huge thank you goes to the Pupil’s Committee for organising such a successful event, as well as to all the staff and volunteers who helped plan and supervise the night. We also appreciate everyone who attended and brought such positive energy to the celebration.

A Night to Remember

A popular feature of the evening was the photobooth, where friends took fun and memorable pictures together.

Everybody looked amazing in their formal dresses and smart suits, adding to the glamour of the occasion. A highlight of the night was the crowning of Prom King and Queen — congratulations to Afonso and Melina!

The dance floor was full all evening, with students singing, dancing, and making wonderful memories with friends.

The Snow Ball was truly a special event for everyone.
